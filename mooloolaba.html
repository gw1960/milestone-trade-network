Why roof restoration matters in Mooloolaba

  • Coastal exposure: salt, humidity and strong UV degrade paint and metal fasteners faster than inland locations.
  • Wind and storm impacts: onshore winds and seasonal storms can dislodge tiles, flashings and ridge cappings.
  • Hinterland runoff and slope effects: properties near the Blackall Range and local hinterland experience different moisture and drainage stresses that affect roof substrates.
  • Restoration avoids costly full re-roofs when conducted early — improving weatherproofing, insulation performance and resale value.
