Coastal and hinterland considerations

Little Mountain sits close enough to the coast that salt corrosion can accelerate metal degradation, while nearby hinterland terrain increases tree debris and organic growth on tiles. Our restoration specifications account for:

  • Salt spray and marine corrosion protection for steel roofing systems.
  • High-UV stable coatings to prevent premature chalking and colour loss.
  • Enhanced fastening and flashing solutions for wind uplift resistance during storm events.
  • Biocide treatments and improved overflows to reduce moss, lichen and blocked gutters from eucalypt and subtropical vegetation.
