Why roof restoration is essential in Maroochydore

Maroochydore roofs face a unique combination of coastal salt air, high UV levels, wind-driven rain and occasional storm surge. Nearby coastal suburbs like Mooloolaba, Alexandra Headland and Cotton Tree share these stresses, while the Sunshine Coast hinterland and Blackall Range introduce steep pitches, runoff challenges and erosion risks that affect flashings and gutters.

  • Salt corrosion on metal roofs and flashings
  • UV degradation of tiles and membrane coatings
  • Blocked gutters and stormwater runoff from steep hinterland lots
  • Loose or missing ridge capping after seasonal storms
  • Hidden substrate damage under accumulated debris
