Why Yaroomba roofs need specialised attention

  • Coastal exposure: salt spray and humidity accelerate corrosion on metal roofs and degrade mortar in tiled roofs.
  • High UV and heat cycles: coatings need UV-stable formulations to prevent premature chalking and colour fade.
  • Hinterland wind and storm variability: stronger downdrafts and debris from bushland increase impact and wear.
  • Local roof pitches and access: steep coastal pitches and confined sites require experienced trades and safe scaffold systems.

  • Visible leaks, water stains or sagging in roof cavities after storms.
