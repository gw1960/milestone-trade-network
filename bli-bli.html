Why Bli Bli roofs need specialist attention

  • Coastal corrosion and salt-laden air accelerate metal roof degradation and flashing failure.

Common signs your roof needs restoration

  • Visible rust, flaking paint or corroded fasteners on metal roofs.
  • Discoloured, cracked or slipped tiles and degraded underlay.
  • Recurring leaks after heavy rainfall or storm events.
  • Moss, lichen or biological growth that traps moisture and damages substrate.
  • High energy bills due to poor roof reflectivity and insulation performance.
