Why Castaways Beach roofs need specialist restoration

Coastal and nearby hinterland conditions create unique roof risks. Local restoration addresses these variables to extend service life and reduce costly repairs.

  • Salt spray and coastal corrosion from the Pacific Ocean and nearby dune systems
  • High UV exposure causing paint and protective coatings to break down
  • Intense rain events and storm winds during wet season and cyclone exposure
  • Steep ridgelines and elevated blocks common on coastal escarpments and hinterland slopes
  • Biological growth (moss, lichen) in humid microclimates near coastal vegetation

Signs your Castaways Beach roof needs restoration

  • Worn, faded or flaking roof paint across large areas
  • Loose or cracked ridge tiles, slipped tiles or visible gaps
  • Rust or perforation on metal roof sheets, especially near fasteners
  • Patchy moss, lichen or regular pooling after rain
  • Rising energy costs from reduced reflector properties on roof surfaces
  • Water stains inside ceilings or fresh leaks after storms
