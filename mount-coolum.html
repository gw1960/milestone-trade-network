Signs your Mount Coolum roof needs restoration

  • Faded or chalked paint, peeling coatings and visible rust on metal roofs
  • Broken, slipped or spalled tiles and cracked mortar bedding
  • Moss, lichen growth and blocked gutters from local vegetation
  • Storm or wind damage from coastal squalls and occasional cyclonic conditions
  • Rising energy costs from reduced thermal performance
  • Leaking, water stains or internal damp after heavy hinterland rainfall
