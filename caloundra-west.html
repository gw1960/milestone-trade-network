Why Roof Restoration Matters on the Sunshine Coast

Sunshine Coast roofs face salt-laden sea breezes, high UV, heavy storm rainfall and variable hinterland terrain that accelerates wear. Proper restoration extends roof life, improves energy efficiency and protects against water ingress during seasonal storms and coastal weather events.

Common Signs You Need Roof Restoration

  • Peeling or chalking paint and faded roof colour
  • Rust spots, perforation or corroded seams on metal roofs
  • Broken, slipped or cracked roof tiles and ridge capping issues
  • Algae, moss or lichen growth causing blocked valleys and gutters
  • Leaking, water stains on ceilings or sagging battens
  • Increased energy bills from lost thermal performance
