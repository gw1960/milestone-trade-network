Signs Your Roof Needs Restoration

  • Visible moss, lichen or widespread staining across roof planes.
  • Flaking or chalking paint and corroded metal flashings.
  • Missing or cracked tiles, sagging ridges or displaced capping.
  • Repeated leaks after rain or damp patches in ceilings.
  • Age-related wear: typical restoration interval for tiles/metal roofs in coastal QLD.
