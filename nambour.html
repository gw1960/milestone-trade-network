Why Nambour roofs need specialist restoration

Nambour sits in the Sunshine Coast hinterland where coastal weather and hinterland terrain combine to accelerate roof wear. Salt-laden winds from nearby coastal towns (Maroochydore, Mooloolaba, Noosa) and higher-moisture valleys around the Blackall Range increase corrosion and algae growth. Combined with steep pitches, eaves exposure and seasonal storms, ageing tile and metal roofs require targeted restoration to prevent leaks, structural rot and lost energy efficiency.
